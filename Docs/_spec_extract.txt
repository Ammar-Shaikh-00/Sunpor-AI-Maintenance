Final Tile Descriptions
Dosing / Material
What is recorded?
Record all events related to dosing as well as raw materials and materials used.
Examples:
Dosing deviations, material anomalies, batch problems, pentane, nitrogen, graphite, recyclate, moisture, foreign material, raw material changes, and other material events.
🔵 Record now →
---
Extruder
What is recorded?
Record all events, observations, and interventions on the extruder.
Examples:
Pressure, temperature, torque, screw speed, melt temperature, trend deviations, process instabilities, changed settings, and operator interventions.
🔵 Record now →
---
Screen Changer
What is recorded?
Record all events related to the screen changer.
Examples:
Screen change, screen inspection, differential pressure, blockages, pressure rise, material deposits, maintenance work, and other anomalies.
🔵 Record now →
---
Die / Die Plate
What is recorded?
Record all events related to the die and die plate.
Examples:
Die inspection, die cleaning, die change, die plate cleaning, blocked holes, material exit, deposits, and damage.
🔵 Record now →
---
Water Box
What is recorded?
Record all events related to the water box cooling process.
Examples:
Water temperature, water pressure, water flow rate, cooling problems, leaks, blockages, alarms, and other anomalies.
🔵 Record now →
---
Granulator / Knives
What is recorded?
Record all events related to the granulator and the knives.
Examples:
Knife change, knife sharpening, knife inspection, granulator inspection, poor cut quality, dust content, particle shape, granulator malfunctions, and other anomalies.
🔵 Record now →
---
Quality Data
What is recorded?
Record the results of the quality inspection here and link them with the corresponding production events.
Examples:
Sieve analysis, lumps, open holes, twin beads, particle size distribution, particle shape, bulk density, foaming behavior, as well as releases or holds of batches.
🔵 Record now →
---
General Event
What is recorded?
Record exceptional events here that cannot be clearly assigned to a process station.
Examples:
Power outages, compressed air failures, network malfunctions, PLC malfunctions, HMI malfunctions, fire alarms, safety events, and other general incidents.
🔵 Record now →
---
Recent Entries
What is displayed?
Here you find all already recorded production events and quality inspections. Entries can be searched, filtered, opened, and – where authorized – edited.
Examples:
Show production events, open quality data, search by production run, batch, or operator, filter events, and edit already recorded entries.
